Key Terms

Pre-image: The original figure before a transformation.
Image: The new figure after a transformation (labeled with primes, like A′).
Rigid Motion (Isometry): A transformation (translation, reflection, rotation) that preserves side lengths and angle measures.
Congruent: Figures are congruent if a sequence of rigid motions maps the pre-image exactly onto the image.
